Correctness

All old/new runs kept sign=1, and both trees passed serial and MPI test suites before measurement.

The old/new output diffs are expected to be non-empty. PH symmetry makes the half-filled ntot error column exactly zero, while energy and doublon differences are at the printed last digit and far below the reported jackknife errors.

Interpretation

The PH symmetry path is working as intended. In the measured half-filled, bipartite 16x16 cases, spin-dependent sweep work is almost exactly halved. The next main performance target is the remaining stabilization path inside the single retained spin sector: green_stack_build, green_from_stack, udv_inv_one_plus, and BLAS calls.
