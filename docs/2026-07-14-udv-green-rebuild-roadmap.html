Standalone two-sided solver Done in working tree Implement udv_inv_one_plus_two_sided_work() against dense references. udv_inv_one_plus_two_sided_work() builds H = D_lb^-1 U_l^T T_r^-1 D_rb^-1 + D_ls (T_l U_r) D_rs, returns g = T_r^-1 D_rb^-1 H^-1 D_lb^-1 U_l^T, and computes determinant sign without forming the combined UDV. Tests compare g and det sign against dense (I + L R)^-1, include existing combine agreement for a moderate case, and cover one-sided-large e^600, two-sided-medium e^300 x e^300, graded {e^250,1,e^-250}, combine-fail {e^400,1,e^-400}, and negative determinant cases. Green.rebuild_mode selects the boundary solver. combine keeps the existing udv_combine + udv_inv_one_plus_work path. two_sided calls udv_inv_one_plus_two_sided_work() and no longer enters udv_combine for boundary reconstruction. The temporary main.c runtime guard was removed. Verified by focused Green/DQMC tests, full make test, MPI test binaries, and serial/MPI two-sided smoke runs with finite output.

Gate (a) is complete: reviewer-side validation showed the beta=33.325 reproduction passes the former udv_combine stage=C site and fails later at udv_lmul_work stage=B_U_D, matching the single-factor prediction. DQMC-level two-sided sweep regression coverage was added for forward stack and alternating/backward paths. make test, make test_mpi, and make test_slow pass. beta=30-32 wall-inside runs completed in both modes and matched at printed precision under the local validation setting documented in 2026-07-14-udv-phase5-validation.md.
6. Single-factor scale countermeasure Original solver design returned; Track A investigated Design and implement a countermeasure for single-factor D range overflow. The original dense augmented solver design was returned by review: chunk spread 300-scale loses Green accuracy, while an accurate spread gate makes dense LU too expensive. Dense augmented is now test-oracle only; a production chain path would require structured O(m N^3) elimination. Track A centered scalar offset was investigated on the real trajectory: beta=33.325 has only about 31.9 natural-log units of centered margin, but a captured real factor at spread 1350.77 gave 6.8e-13 relative Green error against a 500-digit reference. A limited Track A prototype is the next candidate; it does not solve beta=100.

Decision Gates

  • Do not make two_sided the default yet. Phase 5 local wall-inside agreement is complete, but beta=33.325 still needs the Phase 6 single-factor countermeasure.
  • Treat the beta=33.325 reproduction completion as a Phase 6 acceptance target; Phase 2.1 confirmed the single-factor D risk rather than removing it.
  • Do not accept green_rebuild=log in the parser until the lmul/rmul log-D input scaling design is complete.
  • Do not add columns to replicas.csv in Phase 1; use stdout header metadata first.
  • Keep fail-fast diagnostics active in all modes, including future two-sided reconstruction.
  • Keep green_rebuild=two_sided opt-in until Phase 6 determines whether the remaining single-factor failure can be removed without unacceptable truncation or rank loss.
